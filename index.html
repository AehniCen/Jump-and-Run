That's Pepe.
Pepe is good at two things in life:
His famous salsa sauce, "Pepe's Burn to Hell" - so hot, no chicken has ever survived it...
...and KILLING CHICKENS!
So when a gigantic chicken epedemic starts terrorizing his village, there's only one man everyone can turn to. Pepe.
Armed with his trusty salsa bottles and his powerful jumping skills, Pepe sets out to fight his way through hordes of tiny and not-so-tiny chickens.
But beware...
At the end of his journey, something much bigger is waiting for him.
